- **Skills that every session finds.** aegis keeps each person's skills beside their memories, one SKILL.md folder each, and the block under a message now names the three skills that may matter too. Agents of every harness find them with `skill_search`, load them with `skill_read` and save them with `skill_save`, and a session's card and sidebar list the skills it loaded. Each skill is also a command in the composer, `/<name> args`, in every harness.
