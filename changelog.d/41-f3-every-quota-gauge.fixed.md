- **F3 and F10 show every quota window of every provider, keep the last reading when fetches fail, and poll Claude a third as often.** F3 kept only each provider's worst window, so two of five bars were on screen, and a provider whose fetches failed for five minutes (Claude's usage endpoint answering 429, say) lost its row in both F3 and F10 with no word on why. Now each window has a bar; the last good reading stays up with `(stale)` for as long as fetches fail; a provider with credentials but no reading yet prints its reason (`cc  rate limited`). Readings are cached in `~/.cache/aegis/quota/` and shared by every aegis process, so a new TUI starts from the last reading, a second TUI adopts the first one's instead of asking again, and a 429 any of them sees backs all of them off. Claude is read every three minutes instead of every minute, and a turn end may not ask sooner than one minute instead of ten seconds. A busy session at 80x40 runs one row past the F3 column and scrolls by that row.
