title: Layouts and the canvas
kind: tutorial
when: a tutorial to practise the canvas step by step after getting started: panes, their layout, and window 2
---

This tutorial arranges a session's canvas: the transcript and the panes of
pages and files docked around it. It assumes you finished getting-started.
The transcript page's canvas sections hold every rule it uses.

## 1. Dock two things

This step spends quota.

In a session, ask "Send me the README with file_send docked on the right,
then send an artifact with three buttons docked below it." You should see two
panes stacked in one column to the right of the transcript, the file above
the page.

## 2. Move and resize

Drag the page's pane by its bar onto the left fifth of the file's pane. You
should see it become a column of its own. Drag the grip on the line between
two columns to resize them; a column stays at least 280 px wide, the
transcript's at least 360 px.

## 3. Let the agent read and set the canvas

This step spends quota.

Ask "Read the canvas with layout_read and tell me what is docked where, then
use layout_set to put both panes in one column on the right." You should see
the agent list the panes, and the canvas change to match.

## 4. Move a pane to window 2

On the page's pane bar, press the button with two arrows, Move to another
browser window, then New window. You should see a new browser tab that holds
only that pane, titled with the session's name and window 2, and a chip at the
top of window 1's canvas saying window 2 · 1 pane. Press bring back on the
chip: the pane returns to window 1.

## 5. Undock

Press × on each pane's bar. You should see each go back to its card in the
transcript, with the transcript filling the tab again.
